Delete Your
kath. Account

We believe you should always have full control of your data. Here's how you can delete your account and what happens when you do.

hover to open · tap to say bye

If you still have
the app installed

  1. 1 Open the Kath app
  2. 2 Go to Settings
  3. 3 Scroll down to the Danger Zone section
  4. 4 Tap Delete My Account
  5. 5 Confirm twice when prompted

Your account and all associated data are deleted immediately and permanently — this cannot be undone.

If you no longer have
the app installed

Email us at meetkathh@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request", from the email address associated with your account.

We will delete your account and data within 7 business days and confirm by email once it's done.

What gets deleted

When your account is deleted, we permanently remove:

  • Your profile, including your name, photos, and preferences
  • Your journal entries, mood logs, goals, and habits
  • Your conversations with Kath
  • Your matches and anonymous chat messages
  • Your login credentials

What's retained

Safety reports you were involved in (as a reporter or a reported user) are kept for trust & safety purposes, but your personal identity is removed from them first — they can no longer be linked back to you.

About your subscription

Deleting your account does not automatically cancel an active Google Play subscription.

If you have a paid plan, please also cancel it separately through the Google Play Store (Play Store app → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions) to stop future billing.
