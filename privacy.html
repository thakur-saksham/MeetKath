Privacy Policy for Kath

Last Updated: July 2026

At Kath, your privacy is important to us. We built Kath to help people form meaningful connections while respecting their personal information.

1. Information We Collect

When you use Kath, we may collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number (if provided)
  • Location (city/state/country)
  • Messages sent to Kath AI
  • Usage statistics
  • Device information
  • Browser type
  • IP address
  • Cookies and analytics data

We do not collect unnecessary personal information.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information helps us:

  • Improve AI conversations
  • Match compatible users
  • Personalize your experience
  • Improve app performance
  • Prevent fraud and abuse
  • Respond to support requests

3. AI Conversations

Kath AI analyzes conversation patterns including:

  • Response timing
  • Conversation style
  • Communication preferences
  • Personality signals

This helps improve compatibility matching. We do not sell your conversations.

4. Data Security

We use industry-standard security practices to protect your data. Although we strive to keep your information secure, no online service is completely risk-free.

5. Anonymous Matching

Kath is designed around anonymous conversations. Your identity is not shared with other users unless you choose to reveal it.

6. Cookies

Kath may use cookies to:

  • Remember preferences
  • Improve performance
  • Analyze traffic
  • Enhance user experience

7. Third-Party Services

Kath may use trusted third-party providers including:

  • Authentication services
  • Payment providers
  • Analytics platforms
  • Cloud hosting providers

Each provider has its own privacy policy.

8. Children's Privacy

Kath is intended for users 18 years or older. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Your Rights

You may request to:

  • Access your data
  • Update your information
  • Delete your account
  • Delete stored conversations
  • Contact support regarding your data

10. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes become effective once posted on this page.
